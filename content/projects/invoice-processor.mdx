title: Invoice Processor
summary: AI-powered, interactive solution to reconcile invoices.
solutionOverview: Multi-stage LLM invoice extractor and processor to supercharge human workflow.
problem: |
  Invoices are extremely difficult to manually verify, due to their length and
  the fact that supporting documentation for any line item may be spread over numerous pages.
constraints:
  - Must keep a human in the loop for conflict resoluation at each stage.
  - Needs to avoid LLM hallucination, even for large inputs.
  - Pipeline had to be able to process encrypted/signed PDFs and scanned images.
architecture: Text extraction + dynamic entity generation -> LLM object matching + manual review -> LLM reconciliation results.
impact:
  - Reduced chances of human error.
  - Improved efficiency of employees verifying invoices.
