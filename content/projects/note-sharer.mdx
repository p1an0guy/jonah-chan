title: Note Sharer
summary: Student-developed, campus-specific marketplace for course resources.
solutionOverview: Employ a credit economy and voting system to uphold integrity, encourage contribution, and eliminate the free-rider problem and the need for paid subscriptions.
problem: |
  Course resources tend to only be available for those with connections, whether through classes or Greek life organizations,
  or those with the means to access paywalled services like Chegg or Brainly.
constraints:
  - Must democratize access to high-quality course resources.
  - Need to be able to operate without charging a subscription free.
  - Has to ensure high-quality uploads and academic integrity.
architecture: Vercel-hosted web app powered by Next.js and Supabase for the backend.

role: Project Tech Lead

  - Supabase Databases + Storage
