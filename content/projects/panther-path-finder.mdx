problem: Campuses experience under-utilization of resources due to difficulty in finding them.

  - Needs to give clear, natural language directions to classrooms, bathrooms.

impact:
  - Save students an estimated 40+ hrs/semester of getting lost.
  - Increase discovery and usage of campus resources.
